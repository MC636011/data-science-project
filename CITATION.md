# Data citation and attribution

This project uses the U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) airline on-time performance statistics. The BTS TranStats portal is the official source:

- U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics. *TranStats: On-Time Performance.* https://www.transtats.bts.gov/

The analysis notebook was prepared from the public mirror:

- Kaggle. *Airline On-Time Statistics and Delay Causes (BTS).* https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/daryaheyko/airline-on-time-statistics-and-delay-causes-bts

Use and redistribution of the data remain subject to the terms displayed by BTS and Kaggle. This repository documents the source and does not claim ownership of the underlying data.
